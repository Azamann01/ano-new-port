Most small and midsize businesses start with spreadsheets, and for good reason: they're flexible, familiar, and free. But there's a point where a spreadsheet stops being a tool and starts being a liability: broken formulas, version conflicts between team members, and processes that only one person fully understands.

## The warning signs

A few patterns tend to show up right before a business outgrows its spreadsheet and email workflow:

1. The same data gets manually retyped into two or three different systems.
2. One person becomes a single point of failure because they're the only one who "knows how the sheet works."
3. Mistakes are discovered days later, after they've already caused a scheduling conflict or a billing error.

## Custom technology doesn't mean enterprise scale

There's a common misconception that custom technology means a massive, expensive system. In practice, most of the tools we build for SMEs that make the biggest difference are small, focused applications that do one job well: a scheduling tool, an intake form, a dashboard that pulls data from two existing systems into one view.

The goal isn't to replace everything you use today. It's to remove the specific manual steps that are costing your team the most time.

## Where to start

If any of the warning signs above sound familiar, the first step isn't a full rebuild. It's mapping out where the manual work actually happens. That's usually enough to spot the most valuable place to start.
